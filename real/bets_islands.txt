Method: islands chosen by bounding box (no other land inside): outlet reaches (NEXT_DOWN=0) of HydroRIVERS; Aisl = sum of outlet UPLAND_SKM in the box, Ri = sqrt(Aisl/pi).
Normalised: a = A/Aisl, l = L/Ri, pooled over islands.
I1: pooled h for big outlets (a in 0.01-0.5) is lower than h for small outlets (a in 1e-4 - 1e-2) by >= 0.05.  (synthetic: big 0.16-0.53 vs small 0.68-0.73)
I2: median l of big outlets (a>=0.05) >= 0.6  (main stream reaches deep into the island).
I3: per island, big-h < small-h on >= 2/3 of islands that have >= 8 big outlets.
